PART A: MACROECONOMICS (40 MARKS)

Q1. Define Involuntary Unemployment. [1 Mark]

Q2. If the Marginal Propensity to Save (MPS) is 0.2, calculate the value of the Investment Multiplier (k). [1 Mark]

Q3. State whether the following statement is true or false, with valid reason: "Ex-ante Savings and Ex-ante Investments are always equal at equilibrium level of income." [1 Mark]

Q4. Categorize the following government receipts into Revenue Receipts and Capital Receipts, giving valid reasons: (i) Recovery of loans (ii) Corporation tax [3 Marks]

Q5. Explain the 'Store of Value' function of money. How has it solved the major limitation of the barter system? [3 Marks]

Q6. Distinguish between **Balance of Trade** and **Balance on Current Account** of Balance of Payments on the basis of components. [3 Marks]

Q7. In an economy, the Consumption function is given as: $C = 100 + 0.6Y$ (where $C$ = consumption expenditure, $Y$ = national income). If planned investment is ₹300 crore, calculate: (i) Equilibrium level of income (ii) Value of saving at equilibrium. [4 Marks]

Q8. Explain the role of the **Repo Rate** and **Open Market Operations** in controlling excess demand (inflation) during an inflationary gap. [4 Marks]

Q9. Explain the steps involved in calculating National Income using the **Income Method**. State any two precautions that must be taken while estimating it. [6 Marks]

Q10. *(Option 1)* Explain the components of Aggregate Demand in a two-sector economy with the help of a suitable schedule or diagram.
OR
*(Option 2)* What is meant by **Deficient Demand**? Explain its impact on output, employment, and general price level, and explain any one fiscal measure to correct it. [6 Marks]

PART B: INDIAN ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT (40 MARKS)

Q1. What was the primary objective of India's Five-Year Plans regarding **Equity**? [1 Mark]

Q2. State the meaning of **Subsidies** in the context of agricultural policy. [1 Mark]

Q3. Name two institutional reforms initiated in Indian agriculture under the Green Revolution era. [1 Mark]

Q4. Discuss any two major drawbacks of the **Licensing Policy** implemented during the industrial planning period (1950–1990). [3 Marks]

Q5. Why is **Human Capital Formation** considered vital for the economic growth of a developing nation? Give three arguments. [3 Marks]

Q6. Distinguish between **Formal sector** and **Informal sector** employment with examples. [3 Marks]

Q7. Explain the main causes of **rural poverty** in India. State any two steps taken by the government for poverty alleviation. [4 Marks]

Q8. State and discuss the salient features of India's **Foreign Trade Policy** during the pre-1991 period (Import Substitution Strategy). [4 Marks]

Q9. Critically evaluate the **New Economic Policy of 1991 (LPG)** with reference to its impact on the agricultural and industrial sectors. [6 Marks]

Q10. *(Option 1)* Discuss the emergence of **Organic Farming** as a sustainable livelihood option for Indian agriculture, highlighting its benefits and challenges.
OR
*(Option 2)* Compare and analyze the developmental strategies and economic growth path of **India and Pakistan** with respect to demographic indicators and sectoral growth. [6 Marks]
