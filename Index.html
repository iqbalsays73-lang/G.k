1857 - 1885: Shuruat aur Pehli Kranti

Mangal Pandey se shuru hui ladai. 1885 mein A.O. Hume dwara Indian National Congress (INC) ki sthapna hui, pehle adhyaksh W.C. Bonnerjee the.

1905 - 1919: Halchal aur Andolan

1905: Bengal Partition (Lord Curzon). 1906: Muslim League bani. 1916: Lucknow Pact (Congress & Muslim League ek hue). 1919: Jallianwala Bagh Massacre.

1920 - 1947: Gandhi Era & Freedom

1920: Non-Cooperation Movement. 1930: Dandi March. 1942: Quit India Movement ("Karo ya Maro"). 1947: 15 August ko Bharat Azaad hua.
